Registration Form

How to enter a team in the Crawley Darts League: download the form, fill it in and send it to the League Secretary.

Team registration form

Two pages to print and fill in using capital letters. It asks for your team's name and home venue, your team secretary and captain, and all your players.

How to register your team

  1. 1Download the form and fill it in

    Fill in every part: your team name, home venue, captain and secretary, and all your players.

  2. 2Send the form and pay the fee

    Send the form to the League Secretary by email or in person. Pay the registration fee by bank transfer, or in person at a committee meeting.

  3. 3Go to the captains' meeting

    Each team must send someone to the captains' meeting before the season, where the fixtures, rules and other important news are discussed.

  4. 4Check your team's details

    When your team appears on this website, check that its details are right.

Team requirements

What every team needs to join the league.

Players

  • At least 8 registered players per team
  • We recommend 10 to 12 players, to cover absences
  • Each team must have a captain and a secretary
  • Mixed teams of men and women are welcome

Venue

  • A suitable venue with at least one dartboard
  • The board must be in good condition and well lit
  • Regulation height (5ft 8in to the bull) and distance (7ft 9.25in)
  • A fixed oche (the line you throw from)
  • Reasonable space around the board for players and spectators

Registration fee

  • Team registration fee: £40 per season
  • In some cases, your venue might be willing to help you with this
  • The fee must be paid by the registration deadline
  • It covers league administration, trophies and the website
  • You can pay by bank transfer, cash or cheque

Which division new teams join

New teams usually start in the A Division, unless the committee agrees otherwise. Promotion to the Premier Division depends on how a team did the season before.

The league season

What happens when, each year.

  1. August: Registration opens for the new season
  2. Early September: Registration closes, and the fees are due
  3. Mid-September: The captains' meeting before the season
  4. Early October: The season starts with the first league matches
  5. November: The cup competitions begin
  6. May to June: The season ends with the presentation night

About the Crawley Darts League

The Crawley Darts League has been running for over 25 years and is one of the most established and competitive darts leagues in the area. We offer:

  • League: two divisions (Premier and A Division) with promotion and relegation
  • Matches: on Friday evenings, with 3 doubles games and 6 singles games
  • Cup competitions: knockout cups throughout the season
  • Special events: singles, pairs and charity tournaments through the season
  • Awards: an end of season presentation, with trophies for the division winners, cup winners and individual achievements
  • Social side: a friendly community of darts players with regular social events

We welcome players of all abilities, from beginners to experienced county players. The league is competitive but friendly, with a strong focus on sportsmanship.
