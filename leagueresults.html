How to read the results

Tick
The team that won the match, also shown in bold.
Walkover
One team did not play, so the other team was given the match. No games were played.
Week of
League matches are on Friday evenings, so results are grouped by match week, from Friday 6pm to the next Friday 6pm, and each week is named after its Friday. A score sent in a few days late still shows in the week the match was played.
