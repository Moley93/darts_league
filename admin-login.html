Record a Forfeit (Walkover)

Use this when a team cancels a league fixture. The opponent is awarded the points as a completed result (e.g. 9-0) with no individual games recorded, so nobody's singles, doubles, 180s or finishes are credited and Player Rankings are unaffected. The forfeiting team's deduction is stored separately and stays in place for the whole season, no matter how many scores are entered afterwards.

Set to 0 for no deduction.

Points Adjustments (season-long)

Manual bonus or penalty points, added on top of points earned from results. They are shown as a footnote under the league table and are only removed by deleting them here (or by a Season Reset). Deductions created by a forfeit are removed automatically if that forfeit is deleted from Match Results.

Knockout Cup Draw

A random draw replaces the existing cup bracket for the chosen division. Every cup match in that division (and its singles/doubles/180s/finishes) is wiped, then a fresh first-round draw is generated. Later rounds are created empty and fill in as winners are submitted from the team dashboards. Bracket size is the next power of 2 above the team count (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64). Leftover slots become first-round byes that auto-advance their opponent.

⚠️ Danger Zone

Full Season Reset

This will permanently delete:

  • All teams and their captain logins
  • All players
  • All league matches, singles results, doubles results
  • The entire knockout cup bracket for both divisions (every cup match, all completed and pending rounds)
  • All 180s and high finishes (from both league and cup)
  • All league standings
  • All player requests (pending, approved, denied)
  • All points adjustments (forfeit deductions and manual bonuses/penalties)
  • All cup competition draws (3-2-1, Singles, Pairs, Good Friday, Paul/Terry, Memorial and Cap/Sec Cups)

Your admin account and any league announcements are not affected. You'll have to add every team and player from scratch afterwards, and run a fresh cup draw if you want a new bracket. There is no undo.
