Match results

  • Edit opens a league result so you can change its games, 180s and high finishes.
  • Delete removes a league result with all its games, 180s and high finishes. The league table and the player rankings update by themselves. There is no undo.
  • A walkover cannot be edited: delete it, then record it again under Forfeits and deductions.
  • Captains enter cup results. Deleting a cup result puts the match back to awaiting a result and takes the winner back out of the next round.

Forfeits and deductions

Record a league match that a team did not play, and add or take away points for the season.

Record a forfeit (walkover)

Use this when a team cancels a league match. The other team gets the points as a finished result (for example 9-0). No games are recorded, so no player gets singles, doubles, 180s or finishes, and the player rankings do not change. The points taken from the team that cancelled stay for the whole season.

From 0 to 50. Use 0 to take no points.

Points adjustments for the season

Bonus or penalty points, added to the points a team gets from its results. They show as a note under the league table and stay until you delete them below (or reset the season). A deduction made by a forfeit goes when that forfeit is deleted from Match results.

Put a minus sign in front to take points away, for example -5.
Everyone sees this under the league table. For example: fielded an unregistered player.

Cups

Choose a cup. The Knockout Cup is played by teams; the other cups by players.

A new draw wipes the old one

Making a draw for a division deletes every Knockout Cup match in that division, with its games, 180s and high finishes, and draws a new first round at random. The later rounds start empty and fill in as captains send their results. The draw is the next size up from the number of teams (2, 4, 8, 16, 32 or 64): the spare places are first-round byes, and a team drawn against a bye goes straight through.

Season reset

For the end of a season only: it clears the league so a new season can start from nothing.

This permanently deletes:

  • all teams and their captains' logins
  • all players
  • all league matches, with their singles and doubles results
  • the whole Knockout Cup draw in both divisions (every cup match, played or not)
  • all 180s and high finishes (league and cup)
  • the league table
  • all player requests (waiting, approved and denied)
  • all points adjustments (forfeit deductions and bonus or penalty points)
  • all cup competition draws (3-2-1, Singles, Pairs, Good Friday, Paul/Terry, Memorial and Cap/Sec Cups)

Your admin login and the league announcements are kept. Afterwards you add every team and player again, and make new cup draws.

There is no undo. You are asked twice before anything is deleted.
