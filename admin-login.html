Knockout Cup Draw

A random draw replaces the existing cup bracket for the chosen division. Every cup match in that division (and its singles/doubles/180s/finishes) is wiped, then a fresh first-round draw is generated. Later rounds are created empty and fill in as winners are submitted from the team dashboards. Bracket size is the next power of 2 above the team count (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64) – leftover slots become first-round byes that auto-advance their opponent.

⚠️ Danger Zone

Full Season Reset

This will permanently delete:

  • All teams and their captain logins
  • All players
  • All league matches, singles results, doubles results
  • The entire knockout cup bracket for both divisions (every cup match, all completed and pending rounds)
  • All 180s and high finishes (from both league and cup)
  • All league standings
  • All player requests (pending, approved, denied)

Your admin account and any league announcements are not affected. You'll have to add every team and player from scratch afterwards, and run a fresh cup draw if you want a new bracket. There is no undo.
